# holdonquietly — Privacy Policy

holdonquietly is a desktop widget that runs locally on your computer.

## What it does
- Reads your local system info (CPU, RAM, GPU, disk, network, battery) and shows it **on your screen only**.
- Controls your current media session (play / pause / skip / volume) locally.
- Optional Discord feature: connects to a shared Discord server via a bot to display the online status of that server''s members. Your Discord user ID, if you enter it in Settings, is saved **only on your computer**.

## Network requests it makes
- Checks GitHub for app updates.
- Fetches your public IP from api.ipify.org to show it as a stat.
- Connects to Discord''s API for the presence feature.

## What it does NOT do
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking.
- No personal data is collected, stored remotely, sold, or shared.

Everything stays on your machine.
